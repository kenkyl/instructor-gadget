Write the lesson for the remediation concept `concept`. `concept.remedy` names the `angle` to take and the `misconception` to attack; `original_explanation` is what the first lesson said (don't repeat it).

- `explanation_md`: 200–350 words of Markdown. Open by stating the wrong belief plainly and showing, concretely, where it fails. Then teach the right model through the `angle`. Use `analogy_domain` only if it helps this angle.
- `example_md`: 120–250 words. One worked example where the wrong belief predicts one outcome and reality gives another; walk through why.
- `diagram_mermaid`: Mermaid source only, no ``` fences, starting with `flowchart`, `graph`, `sequenceDiagram`, `stateDiagram-v2` or `classDiagram`. Contrast the wrong model with the right one if that fits; 4–10 nodes; quote labels with punctuation; no `click`, HTML or styling.
- `citations` and `not_in_docs`: leave empty.
