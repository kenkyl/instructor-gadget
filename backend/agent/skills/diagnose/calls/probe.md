Write **3–4 diagnostic questions** for this learner (§4.2).

- Pitch every question at the learner's **claimed level** (`config.self_level`). The point is to test the claim.
- Each question targets **one concept** the course will depend on early. Name it in `targets_concept`.
- Answerable in **2–4 sentences of free text**. The learner answers all of them on one screen.
- Mix the kinds of evidence: at least one "explain why", and at least one "what happens if…" or "what would you check first…" applied to a concrete situation.
- No multiple choice, no yes/no, no trivia, and no questions that can be answered by restating a definition.
- No hints in the wording.
- Give each question a short stable `id` (`q1`, `q2`, …).
